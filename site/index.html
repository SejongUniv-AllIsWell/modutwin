3DGS Asset Refinement

From overlapping room scans to clean modular assets

Indoor 3DGS assets often contain geometry beyond the intended room boundary. Modutwin removes boundary-crossing Gaussians, reconstructs room surfaces, and prepares assets for reliable downstream alignment.

Automatic Door Corner Extraction

Door geometry becomes the registration cue

Modutwin uses SAM3 to identify doors and extract corner geometry from indoor 3DGS assets. The extracted door rectangle provides a compact, user-verifiable representation for module alignment.

Automatic Door-Based Alignment

Align rooms through shared door structure

Room modules are aligned to the fixed basemap using a door-to-door similarity transform. This keeps independently reconstructed 3DGS assets modular while enabling building-scale navigation.

Door Interaction

Preserve interactive doors after alignment

Door mesh and splat assets remain grouped after registration, allowing the final scene to support open and close interactions instead of flattening doors into static walls.

Future Work

Automatic room boundary surface extraction

A planned PointNet++-based module will infer ceiling, floor, and wall boundary surfaces directly from the Gaussian point cloud to reduce manual refinement.
